GET TO KNOW YOUR ARTIST

Lil Wayne, born Dwayne Michael Carter Jr. on September 27, 1982, in New Orleans, Louisiana,
is one of the most influential rappers in modern hip-hop. Lil Wayne grew up in the
Hollygrove neighborhood of New Orleans. He showed an early interest in music and began rapping as
a child. At just 9 years old, he caught the attention of Bryan “Birdman” Williams, co-founder of Cash Money Records, who
took Wayne under his wing. He later dropped out of high school at the age of 14 to pursue music full time.
He rose to global fame with his Tha Carter album series, especially Tha Carter III, which became a major commercial
success. Beyond his solo career, he founded Young Money Entertainment, helping launch artists like Drake and Nicki Minaj.
