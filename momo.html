GET TO KNOW YOUR ARTIST

Kelvin Momo is a South African DJ, producer, and songwriter known for his smooth, soulful take on amapiano
music. Born Thato Kelvin Ledwaba in Diepkloof, he developed a passion for music at a young age
and began producing while still in school. He rose to prominence in the
late 2010s with projects that showcased his signature style, gaining recognition for his consistency and
musical depth. Some of his well-known albums include Momo’s Private School, Ivy League, and Amukelani,
which earned praise from fans of soulful amapiano.Throughout his career, he has collaborated with several ,
notable South African artists, contributing to the growth of amapiano both locally and internationally. His work has helped
push the genre into new creative directions while maintaining its core identity. Kelvin Momo continues to be
one of the leading figures in amapiano, respected for his originality, consistency, and ability to create deeply atmospheric music.
