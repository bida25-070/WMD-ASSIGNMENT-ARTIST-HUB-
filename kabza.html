GET TO KNOW YOUR ARTIST

Kabza De Small is a South African DJ, producer, and pianist widely regarded as one of the pioneers
of the amapiano genre. Born Kabelo Petrus Motha on November 27, 1992, in Mpumalanga, he
developed a passion for music at a young age and began learning piano early in
life. Kabza De Small rose to fame in the late 2010s when amapiano started gaining
popularity in South Africa.His breakthrough came with hit songs like “Amantombazane” and his collaboration work
with other rising amapiano artists. He later formed the highly successful duo Scorpion
Kings with DJ Maphorisa, releasing several chart-topping projects that helped bring amapiano to international audiences.He is often called the “King of Amapiano” due to his major influence, production skill, and role in popularizing the genre across Africa and the world.
