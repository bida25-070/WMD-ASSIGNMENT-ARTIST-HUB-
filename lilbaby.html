Georgia,USA also known professionally as Lil baby, an

charts and was the best seling albumin 2020. Lil baby won his first Grammy Award
at the 64th annual in 2022. He won in the category of Best Melodic Rap Performance for his contribution to the song "Hurricane" with Kanye West and the weekend. He also won multiple Bet Awards and MTV awards, won Named Artist Of The Year in 2020 by Apple Music
